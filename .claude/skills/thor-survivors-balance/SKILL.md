# Balance check (thor-survivors)

Adapted from Claude-Code-Game-Studios (MIT), see .claude/CCGS-LICENSE.txt.

The reference is Vampire Survivors; the numbers come from REA, so a value that differs from VS is suspect, not
automatically wrong. Measure, do not eyeball. Absence of data is a finding: if a number could not be measured, say
`NOT MEASURED` for it instead of guessing.

## Where the numbers live

- `thor-survivors/app/src/main/assets/game_data.json`: `weapons[KEY]` is an array of rows (row 1 is absolute, later
  rows are deltas; `interval` ms, `power`, `area`, `amount`, `critChance`), `enemies` (`maxHp`, `speed`, `power`,
  `xp`), `stages[KEY]` is a list of minute rows (`minute`, `minimum`, `frequency` in ms, `enemies`, `bosses`, optional
  `treasure`), with the stage `mods` (`EnemyHealthMultiplier`, `GoldMultiplier`, `XPBonus`) in row 0 only, `powerups`
  (shop, `price`, per-rank delta). In Java: `StageDef.rows` (`WaveRow.frequencyMs`, `treasure`).
- Formulas: `game/Sim.java` (damage, crit, knockback, gem and gold pickup, `openChest`), `game/PlayerStats.java`
  (passives and shop to multipliers; note the `cooldownMul` floor of 0.25), `game/Progression.java` (`xpToNext`),
  `game/OfferGenerator.java` (level-up offers), `game/MetaProgress.java` (shop prices and rank).

## Heroes (design phase 3 and 4)

`new Sim(d, stage, starter, meta, "BASTION")` adds a hero and its main weapon (`starter` null = main weapon only); without the hero
argument the VS rules are unchanged. Main weapons and branch tiers are data: `ashfall_data.json` `mainWeapons` (`params`, tier `mods`).
Bot pattern in `HeroAcceptanceTest`: set `in.aimStickX/Y` to the nearest enemy every step, walk in circles, immortal for kills,
standing still and mortal for survival; pass `s.main.def`/`s.main.tier`/offer level to `OfferGenerator.generate` for branch cards.
Measured (3 seeds, 5 min, kills): Whip baseline about 1000, hero main weapon alone about 1200 to 1900, so the main weapon beats
the whole early VS build; consider lowering main weapon power when tuning.

## Ashfall roster, stages and statuses (design phases 5 to 7)

- Enemies and stages of Cinder Flats (`FLATS`) and Deep Mines (`MINES`) are data in `ashfall_data.json` (`enemies`: archetype, `params`, HP in Z units of the
  ZOMBIE row; `stages`: wave rows, `mods`, treasure copied from the VS boss minutes). It is generated by `tools/build_ashfall_stages.py`: change the tables
  there and re-run it, never hand-edit the JSON. Mines `mods` = the library's with health x1.25, speed x1.05. `FOREST` / `LIBRARY` still load for old runs.
- Tuned numbers so far (all approximations, soak with `StageSoak`, see `thor-survivors-soak`): sergeant bolt 8 dmg every 3.5 s (burst 3, speed 230), drone bolt
  6 every 2.4 s, barrel blast 50 to enemies and 15 to the hero, Cargo Loader contact 26, Ashfall boss HP ramps from 20% at 0:00 to 100% at `hpRamp` minutes
  (20, The First 29). Bastion mid-build bot survives 30:00 in 4 of 6 seeds on Flats and 3 of 6 on Mines, the naked bot dies in every run (130 to 450 s).
  Spectre (80 HP) with the same bot dies at 3 to 5 min: the ranged cap of 24 and the early sergeants hit the glass cannon hardest.
- Phase 9 and 10 numbers (all in `ashfall_data.json`, generated by `tools/build_ashfall_stages.py`): elites `first` 180 s, `interval` 150 s less `shrinkPerMin` 1% (floor 100),
  HP x6, damage x1.5, size x1.4, speed x0.9, XP x8, an XP pile of `xpPileLevels` 2 levels in 5 gems; `equipmentRules`: rarity values 1.0 / 1.6 / 2.4, a unique counts 3.0, salvage 5 gold
  per point, the rarity roll 60 / 32 / 8 at minute 3 moving 1.2 points a minute to Rare, Luck 20 points per 1.0; boss params per pattern (see `BossAi`). Measure with `StageSoak`
  (`bossTtk` per boss, `eliteAt`, `worn`, survival of the `mid` bot) and the `Equipment`/`Boss`/`Elite` tests. Gear power share (the design's balance target) = the sum of
  `Equipment.value` worn at the end against level, from `Result.worn`.
- Status damage is a fraction of the hit that applied it (`Status.java`: Burn 0.08 x hit per 0.33 s per stack, Electrify 0.5 x hit x stacks per tick,
  Decay 0.06 x hit per s per stack and -4% enemy damage per stack). Garlic and Corrosive Flask apply Decay, Slag Cannon Burn, Arc Coil Electrify; a Garlic
  standing-still run can now outlive 6 minutes (`HeroAcceptanceTest` allows 9). Check that a Burn-only build does not out-damage a Might build by more than
  25% (design 14, phase 5) when you balance: that comparison is NOT MEASURED yet.

## Method

1. Scope: weapons, enemies/waves, progression, or economy (default: all, shallow).
2. Write a throwaway JVM test `thor-survivors/app/src/test/java/se/felixarvid/thorsurvivors/game/ScratchBalanceTest.java`
   (delete it afterwards, never commit it unless the owner wants a regression test). Pattern from `SimSmokeTest` and
   `WeaponBehaviourTest`: `TestData.load()`, `new Sim(d, d.stages.get("FOREST"), "WHIP", new HashMap<>())`,
   `s.rng.setSeed(n)`, step `s.update(1f/60f, input)`, keep the player alive with `s.hp = s.maxHp`, drain
   `s.pendingLevelUps` with `OfferGenerator.generate(...)`. Write results to `$SCRATCH_OUT` (fallback
   `app/build/scratch/`), one CSV per measurement, not to stdout.
3. Run: `cd thor-survivors && ./gradlew --offline testDebugUnitTest --tests '*ScratchBalanceTest*' --rerun -q`. Always
   `--offline` (no network in the container) and a `--tests` filter that excludes other agents' scratch tests (or work
   in a separate worktree), else their half-written files break your run. Use 3+ seeds and report the median; a
   single seed is an anecdote. A full 30-minute Sim run takes about 3 s, so a matrix of runs is cheap.
   Harness lessons:
   - `Sim` is final: you cannot subclass or hook it. Measure damage by watching enemy HP by id between steps; count a
     dying enemy's last HP as damage too.
   - Re-pin dummies (x, y, `kvx`, `kvy`) every frame: knockback drifts them. A single stationary dummy misleads for
     facing-based weapons (whip, knife, wand); use a ring or a crowd.
   - Drain `pendingChests` with `openChest()` as well as `pendingLevelUps`. To keep the player alive set
     `s.hp = s.maxHp` AFTER `update` (record damage taken first).
   - Survival cannot be measured with naive bots (random walk or standing still). Write `NOT MEASURED` for it.
4. Measurements worth taking:
   - Weapon DPS: weapon alone at level 1..max against stationary 1e5-HP dummies (`WeaponBehaviourTest.dummy`), damage
     dealt per second. Repeat for each evolution and for the pair (weapon + its `evoSynergy` passive).
   - Dominant builds: for each starter, auto-pick level-ups (random seeded policy and "greedy by weapon") for the full
     stage with the player invulnerable; record kills, level reached, time when enemy count first exceeds 200.
   - Survival curve: same without `s.hp = s.maxHp`, standing still and walking; time of death per starter.
   - Enemy vs player: per wave row, enemy HP x `EnemyHealthMultiplier` against measured DPS at that minute (TTK for
     a typical enemy; bosses separately); contact damage per second against `maxHp` and `armor`.
   - XP curve: minutes to reach level 10/20/30/40 and gems collected; flag a dead zone (no level-up for > 90 s) or
     a spike (3+ levels at once outside chests).
   - Checklist, report but do NOT "fix" (this is Vampire Survivors data): the fallback "Gold Coins" level-up card when
     everything is maxed or no offer is left, and the XP flood in the last two minutes (huge level-up spike at the end).
   - Economy: gold per run per stage, runs needed to buy each `powerups` rank (`MetaProgress.nextPrice`); an item
     never worth buying, or a catalog that is exhausted in a few runs, is a finding.
5. Compare with VS expectations only where the data shows them (REA values); mark other targets as `no target`.

## Output

```
## Balance check: <scope>   Health: HEALTHY | CONCERNS | CRITICAL | NOT ASSESSED
Method: seeds, durations, harness file (path), what was not measured.

| # | Severity | Item | Measured | Expected | Proposed change (file, key, old -> new) |
```
- CRITICAL: a build/weapon that dominates every alternative, an unwinnable or auto-won stage, a gold loop with no sink.
- CONCERNS: outliers a tuning pass fixes. Every row carries a concrete number and which measurement supports it.
- Then "Degenerate strategies", "Progression curve" (small table by minute), "Economy" (runs to afford each rank).

Do not edit `game_data.json` yourself in this skill; hand the table to `implementer`. `game_data.json` is generated
by `tools/build_data.py` from REA, so a permanent change belongs in the generator or in a post-processing step, not
only in the JSON. After a change, re-run the same harness and the project tests.
