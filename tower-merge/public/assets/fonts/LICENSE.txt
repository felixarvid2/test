Fonts bundled with Tower Merge - Last Stand
===========================================

kenney-future.ttf   "Kenney Future"  (display font: titles, headings, numbers, button labels)

Part of the Kenney "UI Pack" (2.0) by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Free for personal, educational and commercial use; credit is appreciated but not required.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack

Body text (descriptions, small print) deliberately uses the platform's bold UI font
(system-ui / Roboto / Helvetica / Arial) so long sentences stay readable and the download
stays small; nothing else is bundled.
