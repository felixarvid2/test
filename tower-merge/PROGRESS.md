# Progress

## Status

All six phases are implemented. The game is playable in a browser and packages for YouTube Playables. It has not been tested on a real phone.

## Completed

### Phase 1: Foundation

- Vite, TypeScript, Phaser 3.90 and vitest project, with shared contracts in `src/data/types.ts` and `src/sim/api.ts`.
- Headless pure-TypeScript simulation in `src/sim/`, with no Phaser dependency.
- 720x1280 portrait world, FIT-scaled to any aspect ratio.
- Serpentine enemy path, flyers cut diagonally. Castle at the bottom.
- 12 slots, 4 open at the start. Locked slots are bought with gold.
- Buy rolls a random tower from the loadout. Drag-to-merge onto an identical tower (max level 5).

### Phase 2: Content

- 8 towers, 5 levels each. Levels 2–5 add a perk.
- 10 enemy types, plus boss spawns stoneling and imp. Skeletons have a front shield, bats fly, shamans heal, summoners spawn.
- 3 bosses with 2–3 phases and telegraphed attacks: Goblin King (wave 5), Stone Golem (wave 10), Dragon (wave 15). They loop with scaling.
- 46 upgrades in 4 weighted rarities (16 common, 13 rare, 11 epic, 6 legendary). One is picked after each wave.
- 20 data-driven synergies.
- 5 random events: Treasure, Elite, Merchant, Mystery Chest, Cursed.
- Abilities: Meteor and Repair from the start, Freeze after a best wave of 5, Frenzy after a best wave of 10.
- Effects are composable data. About 35 flags, with caps: chance cap, slow cap, proc recursion guard, chain cap 25.

### Phase 3: Meta progression

- Gold economy and 10 permanent upgrades.
- Tower unlocks for gems (lightning, fire, poison, sniper, magic). Arrow, cannon and frost are free.
- Tower loadout of up to 4 towers (at least 3, must include an anti-air tower). Buy only rolls loadout towers.
- Versioned save (schema v1) with migrate and repair, debounced writes. `ytgame` saveData in Playables, localStorage in browsers.
- Lifetime statistics, tower collection and bestiary.
- 32 achievements.

### Phase 4: Rewards and platform

- `RewardProvider` interface with Mock, YouTube and Null providers. `RewardService` with idempotent, nonce-checked claims persisted in the save, and daily caps.
- Rewards: placements revive (once per run), rareTower (once per boss), reroll (once per upgrade screen), doubleGems (once per run), emergencyPower (once per run).
- The game loop and audio pause during ads. No reward is needed to progress.
- `BrowserPlatform` and `YouTubePlatform` adapters. The SDK script loads first. Pause only through `ytgame` onPause and onResume. Phaser's visibility listener is dropped inside Playables.
- `YouTubeRewardProvider` is behind `REWARD_CONFIG.youtubeAdsEnabled = false`. Without ads, the YouTube build offers a gem price instead.

### Phase 5: Polish

- Original SVG art generated by Node scripts in `tools/art/`, in one style documented in `docs/ART_STYLE.md`. Procedural fallback textures cover any missing key.
- CC0 audio: Kenney packs and Abstraction music loops (see `CREDITS.md`).
- Gameplay views in `src/game/play/`: BoardView, TowerView, DragController, MergeFx, EnemyView, ProjectileView, Effects, DamageNumbers, Telegraphs, Hud, ShopBar, BossBar, Tutorial.
- Pooled sprites, capped particles, flashes and damage numbers.
- Deterministic daily challenge with 2 of 14 modifiers.
- Menus, overlays and settings.

### Phase 6: Testing and packaging

- 467 unit tests (vitest).
- Headless balance simulation (`npm run sim`) with bot policies and loadout probes. See `docs/BALANCE.md`.
- `npm run smoke`: Playwright end-to-end run covering menu, play, mouse and CDP touch-drag merges, waves with upgrades and events until defeat, game over, play again, menu, daily, on 3 viewports including landscape. Fails on console errors.
- `npm run perf` and the `?stress=1` debug scenario.
- `npm run package` builds, validates the Playables rules and writes `release/tower-merge-playables.zip`.

## Verification

- Unit tests: 467 passing.
- `npm run smoke`: passes with zero console errors. Takes about 8 minutes.
- Production build: passes, including the sprite manifest and typecheck.
- Playables package: 165 files, 1.38 MiB.
- Performance, simulation: 0.18 ms average and 0.49 ms p95 per update with 150 enemies and 12 level-5 towers. About 3 KiB of garbage per update.
- Frame rate is measured only in headless software rendering.
- Balance, greedy bot, median wave: fresh save 11 (about 5.6 min), mid meta 16, late 21, maxed 26. Never-merge policy reaches 6 on a fresh save. Dragon kills 23% of mid-meta runs that reach it.
- Loadouts: all 70 four-tower loadouts analysed. No dominant loadout, and every tower appears in a competitive one.
- Playables API: `ytgame.ads.requestRewardedAd` verified available on 2026-10-18.

## Known limitations / not done

- No real phone has been tested. Touch is tested through CDP emulation.
- Frame rate is measured only in headless software rendering.
- Audio picks have not been listened to by a human. The weakest are `shoot_cannon` and `boss_roar`.
- Synergy chips are 66 px tall but their hit areas are 51 px wide.
- Package warnings: the Phaser chunk is above 512 KiB, and Phaser runs one `new Function()`.
- YouTube rewarded ads are off (`REWARD_CONFIG.youtubeAdsEnabled = false`). They must be enabled in the Developer Portal first.
- Generated AI art was not possible: the fal.ai credential was rejected. All art is original SVG.
- The dev-only overlay harness (`?overlay=<name>`) is excluded from production builds.
