Topology

Drawn from shared subnets and interface descriptions. Red links have problems (adjacency failures, trunk or link mismatches). Click a device to show its routing table below.

Path tester

Simulates a packet hop by hop through the loaded devices (routing tables, ACLs, NAT, VTY lines) – and the reply on the way back.

Devices

Links

Layer-3 links are found from shared subnets. Layer-2 links are guessed from interface descriptions that name another loaded device (e.g. "Trunk to SW2 Gi0/1").

Estimated routing tables

Calculated from connected interfaces, static routes and OSPF/EIGRP/RIP adjacencies that would form with these configs. BGP routes and VRFs are not simulated.
